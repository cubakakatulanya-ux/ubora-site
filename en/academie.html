The context in the DRC

What we see in the field

Training given once, in a classroom, for the length of a project, is soon forgotten. The Académie makes it last: accessible anywhere, at the learner's own pace, and recognised.

  • Training that ends with the projectNew members are never trained.
  • Training in remote areas is costlyAnd rural areas often have no network coverage.
  • Skills that are hard to proveA trained treasurer has nothing to show for it.
The Academy's subject areas

More than 140 courses in 26 learning paths, across seven subject areas

Savings groups (VSLAs)

From member to manager

Setting up, managing, facilitating and overseeing VSLAs, from day one to self-reliance: members and committee, facilitators, trainers, managers.

Income-generating activities

Income-generating activities

Choosing, launching and growing a small business: idea and market, costs and prices, accounts, credit, processing, formalisation.

Financial literacy

Household and business money

Budgeting, saving, credit, negotiation, digital finance.

Entrepreneurship

Starting and growing a business

From the customer's problem to a product that sells: starting a business from scratch, understanding the Congolese ecosystem.

Agriculture and value chains

Agricultural businesses

Building a profitable agricultural business and succeeding in its value chain: food crops, coffee, cocoa, oil palm, livestock, fish farming, and agri-food case studies.

Cooperatives

Producer organisations

Running a cooperative: governing bodies, finances, group sales, business plan.

Supporting others

Coaches, incubators, programmes

Coaching entrepreneurs, running an incubator, designing a support programme, providing support in the field.

Our method

Step by step

  1. 01

    Identifying needs

    Who to train, in what, and at what level.

  2. 02

    Designing the courses

    Short lessons, Congolese examples, exercises with real figures.

  3. 03

    Training online and in the classroom

    Online with no network needed, in person for hands-on practice.

  4. 04

    Certifying

    Timed quizzes, final test, QR-code certificate.

  5. 05

    Training the trainers

    Trainer learning path, micro-teaching, improvement plan.
