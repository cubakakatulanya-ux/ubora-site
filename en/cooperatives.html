Ubora Coop

Well-run cooperatives, and producers paid on time.

A producer group without statutes or a dormant cooperative: we start from what exists, and help it manage, sell and access finance. From inputs to paying the producer.

The context in the DRC

What we see in the field

We rarely start from a blank page: there is almost always a group already working together, or a cooperative that has gone dormant.

  • No legal statusNo statutes, so no account, no contract, no credit.
  • Volumes nobody knowsWithout a register, it is impossible to give a buyer a reliable quantity.
  • Late paymentsPaid late, the producer sells elsewhere the next season.
Two starting points

We always start from what exists

A producer group without statutes

Founding meeting, OHADA statutes, registration, opening a bank account.

A dormant cooperative

Assessment, revised statutes, renewed governing bodies, accounts rebuilt.

Value chain

The whole value chain, from field to payment

A cooperative gains from every link it controls: buying together, storing rather than selling cheap, processing to sell at a higher price. We strengthen the links that pay most.

  1. 01

    Inputs

    Seeds, fertiliser and tools bought as a group, on credit if needed.

  2. 02

    Production

    Good agricultural practices, season calendar, plot monitoring.

  3. 03

    Collection

    Collection points, verified weighing, a record per producer.

  4. 04

    Storage

    Warehousing, drying, preservation, inventory credit (warrantage).

  5. 05

    Processing

    Hulling, milling, packaging, labelling.

  6. 06

    Sales

    Group sales contracts with buyers, through Ubora Market.

  7. 07

    Payment

    Statement per producer, payment by mobile money.

Our method

Step by step

  1. 01

    Assess

    Statutes, governance, accounts, activity.

  2. 02

    Formalise

    OHADA statutes, registration, national identification.

  3. 03

    Organise

    Internal rules, separate roles, cash and stock procedures.

  4. 04

    Manage and sell

    Registers, collection, stock, accounts, group sales.

  5. 05

    Pay and monitor

    Producer statements, mobile money, support for general meetings.
