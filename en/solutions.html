Ubora Fin

Credit, where it never used to reach.

Credit exists in the DRC, but it rarely reaches small borrowers. Ubora Fin does not lend money: it prepares them, presents them to financial institutions and mobilises capital on their behalf.

What we do
  • Train
  • Prepare the application
  • Make the connection
  • Mobilise capital
  • Monitor repayment

Discover Ubora Fin
