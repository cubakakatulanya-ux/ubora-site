Ubora Fin

Connecting those who need credit with those who lend.

Credit exists in the DRC, but it rarely reaches small borrowers. Ubora Fin does not lend money: it prepares them, presents them to financial institutions and mobilises capital on their behalf.

The context in the DRC

What we see in the field

On one side, borrowers with nothing to show. On the other, institutions that lose money on small loans. Both sides need work.

  • No track record, no creditThree years of savings stay invisible until something proves them.
  • Appraisal costs that are too highAppraising a 500-dollar loan costs almost as much as one of 50,000.
  • Arrears spotted too lateWithout monitoring, late payments come to light when it is hard to act.
Our method

Step by step

  1. 01

    Train

    Financial education, credit management, bookkeeping.

  2. 02

    Prepare the application

    Accounts, a costed business plan, realistic collateral, a track record drawn from our tools.

  3. 03

    Make the connection

    Presentation to MFIs, COOPECs and banks, and follow-up of the discussions.

  4. 04

    Mobilise capital

    From financial institutions, impact investors and donors. They provide the finance; we prepare and monitor.

  5. 05

    Monitor repayment

    Due dates, reminders, support in case of difficulty.

Our role as intermediary

The link between funders and savings groups

A bank cannot appraise hundreds of small, remote applications. Ubora Fin provides the link, without lending itself.

Financial institutions, investors, donors Ubora Fin VSLAMembers

For financial institutions

A trained and monitored client base, with no case-by-case appraisal cost, backed by the members' joint guarantee.

For VSLAs

More money to lend, at the right time, without physical collateral, at a reasonable cost.

For donors and investors

A revolving fund rather than a grant spent once, and measured impact.

In preparation

A credit company for VSLAs

To take this role to a larger scale, within a licensed framework, we are preparing a credit company dedicated to VSLAs, built on their savings history and collective discipline.

Feasibility study, regulatory framework and search for financial partners under way.
