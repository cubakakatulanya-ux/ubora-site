What ASBL Manager does

All of an organisation's operations, with no double entry

01

Governance and members

Memberships, dues, general meetings and board, remote voting, minutes; articles of association, internal rules and procedures manual drafted from your settings.

02

Work plan (PTBA)

Activities linked to the results framework, terms of reference, activity sheets and progress tracking.

03

Finance and accounting

Expenses signed off according to amount, advances to be accounted for, cash in francs and dollars, accrual and cost accounting, full financial statements.

04

Projects and donors

Each donor's rules checked on every expense, tranche requests, reallocations, audit-ready file, project close-out; an online monitoring space for the donor.

05

Logistics and staff

Purchasing, procurement and stock; recruitment, contracts, attendance, payroll and staff appraisal; mail and archives; certificates of service and of satisfactory performance.

06

Monitoring, evaluation and reporting

Logical framework, sex-disaggregated indicators, Kobo import, formatted narrative reports, complaints; an AI assistant to help draft documents and spot funding calls.
