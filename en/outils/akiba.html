What AKIBA does

Accounts the whole group can check

  • Guided meeting in 8 steps, three-key closing
  • Sealed register: any change is visible
  • Receipts printed or sent by WhatsApp
  • “MFI-ready” level and a file to present
  • MFI loans tracked from start to finish
  • Akiba Suivi for facilitators and organisations
  • No signal needed: GPS location, meeting sent in one or two images
  • 3 MB Android app, prints on POS terminal printers
  • DRC languages and audio guide
  • Free demonstration; Ubora code for a real VSLA

Before and after

What changes with AKIBA

The group keeps its rules and its meetings. Only the bookkeeping changes, and everyone can now check it.

With the notebookWith AKIBA
BookkeepingA notebook copied out at every meetingEntries on a phone, even without a signal
Share-out calculationSeveral hours, and often disputesImmediate, based on each member's savings
TransparencyOnly the treasurer knows the balancesEach member sees their own position
Loan monitoringLate payments are spotted lateDue dates and alerts for the facilitator
FraudA page torn out, a figure altered, and nobody noticesA sealed register: any change is visible
Member receiptNothing, or a figure written by hand in the passbookA receipt printed or sent by WhatsApp, verifiable against the log
LanguageAccounts in French, which not everyone can readThe app in Lingala, Swahili, Kikongo and Tshiluba, with an audio guide
Access to creditNo record outside the groupAn “MFI-ready” level and a file to present to one
Programme monitoringData that is slow to collect and often incompleteIn Akiba Suivi: dashboard, Impact screen and Excel export for the NGO or donor
