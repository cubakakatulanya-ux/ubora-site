Ubora Hub in four minutes

What Ubora Hub does

From application to report, in a single tool

01

Select

Online calls for applications, application form, selection panel and ranking.

02

Structure the programme

Programmes and cohorts in four phases: ideation, incubation, business launch, then upgrading of established SMEs.

03

Train

Modules, exercises and quizzes that work offline, and built-in training for coaches and directors.

04

Supporting others

Coaching sessions guided module by module, action plans and a shared calendar.

05

Build the business plan

Each entrepreneur builds their business plan in the generator, linked to their file.

06

Report

Dashboard, indicators and PDF reports for management and donors.
