What the business plan generator does

Calculated figures, advice on your project

  • A step-by-step path, tailored to the project from eight answers
  • Sales with seasonality, investments, loans and working capital requirements
  • Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow calculated month by month
  • Break-even point and three scenarios, with reverse calculations
  • Viability score: “Can my project get started?”
  • Targeted advice: phasing investments, loan duration, agriculture module
  • In Congolese francs or US dollars; printable as PDF or exportable to Word
