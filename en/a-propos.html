About us

A social enterprise, so that families' hard work bears fruit.

Ubora means “excellence” in Swahili: it is what we owe our clients, who cannot afford mistakes.

MissionOur business modelOur valuesSupport usQuestions
Mission

Helping Congolese businesses and communities withstand shocks, and then grow.

Most businesses in the country remain informal and far from credit: at the slightest shock, they lose what they have built. We strengthen what makes them resilient: organisation, savings, credit, skills and tools suited to conditions on the ground.

Our business model

Why we chose to be a social enterprise

A two- or three-year project ends just as progress begins. We wanted to be able to stay.

Who pays

The organisations that commission our work: NGOs, donors, financial institutions, programmes.

Who benefits

Savings groups, young people, women and rural producers, at reduced rates.

What we mobilise

Through Ubora Fin, funders' capital for the groups we support. Ubora Fin does not lend money.

What we reinvest in

Our tools, community facilitators and follow-up after projects end.

Frequently asked questions

You may be wondering

Who can work with Ubora?

Savings groups, entrepreneurs, cooperatives and financial institutions, as well as the NGOs, incubators and donors that support them.

Where do you work?

Throughout the DRC, on site or remotely.

Do your tools work without the internet?

Yes. AKIBA, Ubora Hub, downloaded Académie courses and the business plan generator can all be used without a network connection; data is sent as soon as the connection returns.

Do you offer training on your tools?

Yes. Each tool comes with training and follow-up. The Académie also offers more than 140 online courses with certificates.

Do you need to be a registered business to receive support?

No. We also support entrepreneurs and groups on the path to formal registration.

What happens to support when an NGO's project ends?

We stay: follow-up continues after the project closes, funded partly by our own revenue, and the tools remain in use.

Can Ubora finance our savings group?

Ubora does not lend money. Through Ubora Fin, we mobilise capital from financial institutions, investors and donors, and we introduce the group to an MFI, a COOPEC or a bank.

What does Ubora do for the environment?

Through Ubora Green: reforestation, recycling, agroecology and clean energy, with income for those who carry out the work.

How much does support cost?

It depends on the programme and the number of participants. Services to organisations fund reduced rates for groups and micro-entrepreneurs.
