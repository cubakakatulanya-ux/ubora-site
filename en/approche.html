Our approach

Training without tools is not enough. Tools without support are not enough either.

Too many projects train people and then leave; too much software is forgotten six months later. Our method has six steps, and we stay until the results hold.

Our methodDonors and NGOsThe pilot projectImplementationOur servicesAfter the project
The Ubora method

Six steps, always in the same order

For a savings group, an entrepreneur or a cooperative: only the content changes.

  1. 01

    Listen

    On site, with the people concerned, in their own language. We look at what already exists before proposing anything.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Putting in writing what was unclear: who decides, who does what, where the money goes.

  3. 03

    Train

    Everyone learns what they need to make decisions on their own. And community facilitators remain after we leave.

  4. 04

    Equip

    A simple tool replaces the notebook: no network needed, in francs and dollars.

  5. 05

    Connect

    The records kept in our tools become a track record for funders and buyers.

  6. 06

    Follow up

    Visits, shared indicators, answers on WhatsApp. We only leave once things hold.

Donors, NGOs and programmes

Your programmes deserve to last longer than their funding

We implement them in the field, track their results in real time, and then take over.

Start with a pilot project

The safest way to start working with us. The pilot is entrusted to the social enterprise (as a service contract) or to the UBORA association (as a grant).

  1. 01

    Define the scope together

    Target group, area, duration and budget, tailored to you. Indicators are set before we start.

  2. 02

    Launch in the field

    Our teams structure, train and equip; data is recorded from day one.

  3. 03

    Track in real time

    You have access to dashboards and data exports, with a mid-term review.

  4. 04

    Decide on the evidence

    A final report: indicators achieved, lessons learned, and what scaling up would require.
