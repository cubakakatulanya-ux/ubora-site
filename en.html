The Ubora method

From listening to follow-up, in six steps.

For a savings group, an entrepreneur or a cooperative: only the content changes. We stay until the results hold.

  1. 01ListenOn site, with the people concerned, in their own language. We look at what already exists before proposing anything.
  2. 02StructurePutting in writing what was unclear: who decides, who does what, where the money goes.
  3. 03TrainEveryone learns what they need to make decisions on their own. And community facilitators remain after we leave.
  4. 04EquipA simple tool replaces the notebook: no network needed, in francs and dollars.
  5. 05ConnectThe records kept in our tools become a track record for funders and buyers.
  6. 06Follow upVisits, shared indicators, answers on WhatsApp. We only leave once things hold.
Our approach in detail
Our commitments

A business that is accountable.

Our clients pay for a service: they know what they are buying, and what happens to the surplus.

  • A written quotation before every assignmentPrice, timescales and deliverables are set in writing before we start.
  • Reduced ratesServices to organisations fund reduced rates for groups and micro-entrepreneurs.
  • Surpluses reinvestedIn our tools, in training community facilitators and in our presence in the field.
  • Results monitored in real timeIndicators set together, data in our tools from day one.
  • Tools that stayAt close-out, the groups, trained facilitators, tools and records stay in place.
  • Donations kept separateThe business lives on its services. Donations and grants go to the UBORA association, a separate legal entity.
